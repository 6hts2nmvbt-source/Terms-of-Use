# Privacy Policy

**The Set** (the "App")
Last updated: September 28, 2026

---

## 1. Who we are

For any question about this policy or your data, write to ***thesetapplication@gmail.com***

## 2. In short

- The App has **no accounts**. You are never asked to sign up, and we do not collect your name.
- Your call sheets, work hours, notes and attachments **stay on your phone**.
- When you ask the App to read a document, **that document is sent to an AI service**
  (Anthropic) that extracts the data and returns it. This is the only thing the App sends
  anywhere on its own, and only when you ask.
- **We show no ads, we do not profile anyone, and we do not sell data.**

## 3. What stays on your phone

The App stores the following in its private area on your device, which only you can access:

- the call sheets you load or create, with all their content;
- which person you are on a call sheet, so the App can highlight your times;
- your work log: the days you record, your actual start and end times, breaks and notes;
- your personal notes;
- PDFs you attach (for example script pages), and any email address you type in for a
  production manager;
- counters of how many readings and exports you have used this month;
- your preferences: theme, language, maps and messaging apps, alerts.

This data is **not sent to us**, and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you delete the item
or uninstall the App. If you use iCloud Backup, it may be included in your device backup,
which Apple manages under its own terms.

## 4. Automatic reading of call sheets

When you use the reading feature, the PDF, photo or text you choose is sent over an encrypted
connection to **Anthropic PBC**, a company based in the United States that provides the Claude
AI model. It is sent only to extract the document's data and return it to the App. The same
applies to a transport list you load.

What you should know:

- The document is sent **in full**, including any parts you may not need.
- Under Anthropic's commercial terms, content sent through its API is **not used to train its
  models**.
- Anthropic automatically deletes API inputs and outputs from its systems within a limited
  period (currently 30 days). It may keep them longer where necessary, for example if a
  request is flagged as a violation of its usage policy (up to two years) or where the law
  requires it. The AI service may briefly hold the document in temporary memory while
  processing it, to speed up the request.
- Processing takes place in the **United States**. Transfers of personal data outside the
  European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are covered by safeguards such
  as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, included in Anthropic's Data
  Processing Addendum.
- Anthropic describes its practices at **privacy.claude.com**.

**Nothing is sent unless you ask.** Data is transmitted only when you tap the button to load a
document. If you never use that feature, no document leaves your phone.

## 5. Other people's data in call sheets

A call sheet almost always contains the names, roles and phone numbers of other people: crew,
cast, suppliers.

When you load a document, that data is processed as described in section 4. **You decide which
documents to load**, and we ask you to make sure you are entitled to, especially where the
document is covered by a confidentiality agreement with the production.

If you are a production company using the App for work, you may be the controller of your
crew's data. In that case, please contact us to agree a data processing arrangement.

## 6. Sharing and exporting

The App lets you export call sheets and work logs as PDF files, and share them using the
standard iOS share sheet, for example through WhatsApp or email. **You choose the recipient and
the app used**; from that point the file is handled by that app and its own privacy policy, not
by us.

The option to send your work log to a production manager prepares an email in the iOS Mail app,
addressed to the email address you provide, with the PDF attached. **The email is sent by you,
from your own account**: we never send it and never see it.

If you send a PDF to the App from another app through the share sheet, the file is copied to a
private area on your device shared only between the App and its share extension.

## 7. Alerts

If you turn on alerts for your times, the App uses local iOS notifications. They are created
and scheduled **by your phone**: they do not go through any server and tell us nothing. You can
turn them off in the App's settings or in iOS settings.

## 8. Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive or
store your payment card details or your Apple ID: Apple only tells the App whether your
subscription is active. Payments are subject to Apple's terms and privacy policy.

## 9. What we do not do

- We use no analytics, tracking or advertising tools, and no third-party software libraries
  that collect data.
- We do not collect your location. If you open an address in a maps app, you leave the App and
  that app's own terms apply.
- We do not read your contacts. The call and message buttons use the numbers in the call sheet
  and open your phone or messaging app.
- If you choose a photo, the App receives only that image through the iOS photo picker, not
  your photo library.
- We do not share data with third parties, except as described in section 4.

Apple may provide us with aggregated, anonymous diagnostic information, such as crash reports,
if you have chosen to share it with app developers in your iOS settings.

## 10. Security

Data in transit to the AI service is encrypted. Data stored on your device is protected by
iOS's device encryption while your phone is locked with a passcode. No system is completely
secure: keep your device updated and protected.

## 11. Legal bases

Under the GDPR we process data on the basis of:

- **contract** (Art. 6(1)(b)), to provide the reading feature you asked for;
- **legitimate interests** (Art. 6(1)(f)), to keep the service secure and prevent its abuse.

Providing documents is voluntary. If you do not, the reading feature cannot work, but the rest
of the App still does.

## 12. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, object to and port your data.
Because your data stays on your device, you can erase or change it yourself by deleting items
or the App.

For data that passed through the reading service, write to *****Anthropic PBC*. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in Italy this is
the **Garante per la protezione dei dati personali** (www.gpdp.it).

## 13. Children

The App is intended for people working in film and TV production and is **not directed at
children under 16**. We do not knowingly collect children's data.

## 14. US residents

We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are used in US state privacy laws,
and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

## 15. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes,
let you know in the App.
