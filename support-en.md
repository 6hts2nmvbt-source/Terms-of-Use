# The Set — Support

Call sheets you can actually read on your phone.

## Contact

Write to** thesetapplication@gmail.com**  We usually reply within 2 working days.

**Please do not send confidential documents.** If you are reporting a wrong reading, remove or
black out names, phone numbers and anything covered by a confidentiality agreement first, or
describe what went wrong instead of sending the file.

## Common questions

### How do I load a call sheet?
Open the **Today** tab. If no call sheet is open, the loading screen is already there; if one
is open, tap **Load** (the upload icon) at the top left. Choose a PDF or a photo, or paste the
text. The App reads it and shows times, scenes, people, transport and places. If you just want
to look around, tap **Open an example**. Files up to 25 MB are supported.

### Can I load the transport list too?
Yes, from the same screen. The App recognises a transport list by itself and offers to add it
to your call sheet. Loading it does not use up one of your readings.

### Some sections came out empty or wrong
The reading is done by an AI service and **can make mistakes**, especially with very dense
tables or low-quality scans. Always check important times against the original document.
If some sections could not be read, the App tells you which ones. Tap **Edit** (or **Fix**) on
the call sheet to complete or correct them by hand.

### Why doesn't it show my times?
Tap **Who are you?** at the top of the call sheet and pick your name. The App then highlights
your call, pick-up, make-up and scenes.

### How many call sheets can I read?
Without a subscription: 3 readings per month. With **The Set Pro**: 30 per billing period. Only
successful readings count, and a call sheet you have already read never locks: you can always
open it again. **Settings → Readings** shows how many you have used and when the count resets.

### How do I subscribe, restore or cancel?
- **Subscribe:** tap **Go Pro** in Settings, or when the App offers it.
- **Restore:** on the subscription screen, tap **Restore purchases**.
- **Cancel:** in iOS, open **Settings → your name → Subscriptions → The Set Pro → Cancel**.
  Deleting the App does not cancel a subscription.
- **Refunds:** purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at
  https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

### What is the difference between "end of shooting" and "wrap"?
**End of shooting** is when filming stops. **Wrap** is when the set is cleared and the vans are
closed. The App shows both. Overtime in your work log counts from the wrap.

### How is overtime calculated?
It is the time you worked **after the scheduled wrap** on the call sheet, worked out from your
actual start time, so it also works past midnight. If the call sheet has no wrap, the App uses
the end of shooting and tells you so. It is an estimate, not a payroll calculation: check it
against your contract.

### How do I send my work log to the production manager?
Open the day in the **Hours** tab and tap **Send to the production manager**. The App prepares an
email with the PDF attached; you only need to tap Send. If the call sheet has no email for that
person, the App asks you to type it once. To send it another way, use **Export and share**,
which lets you pick WhatsApp, Mail or any other app.

### The alerts don't arrive
Alerts are scheduled on your phone when you open a call sheet, and only for times that are still
ahead. Check that notifications are allowed for The Set in iOS settings, and that the alerts you
want are on in **Settings → Alerts**.

### I opened a PDF from Mail or WhatsApp and The Set isn't in the share sheet
Tap **Share**, scroll the row of apps and choose **The Set**. If you cannot see it, tap **Edit**
at the bottom of the list of apps and move it to the top.

### How do I delete my data?
Swipe a call sheet in **Archive** to delete it. Deleting the App removes everything it stored on
your phone. See our Privacy Policy for what happens to documents sent for reading.

### Which languages does the App support?
Italian, English, French and Spanish. Change it in **Settings → Language**.
